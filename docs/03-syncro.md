# Syncro

See your natural rhythms.

---

## What Syncro is

Syncro is a personal rhythm map, updated every two hours.

Based on your birth context, Syncro reflects how the
current period's energy aligns with your personal
patterns. Where to lean in. Where to slow down. Where
the windows are open — and where they're not.

Think of it as a weather forecast for your inner life.

---

## How Syncro works

You enter your birth date once. Syncro builds a personal
rhythm map drawing from millennia-old observations of
how humans cycle through energy, focus, and direction.

Every two hours, Syncro updates with:

- **The current energy pattern** — what's available right now
- **Aligned directions** — spatial/temporal cues that
  historically resonate with your rhythm
- **Friction periods** — moments when leaning back makes
  more sense than pushing forward
- **Open windows** — times when external conditions
  match your internal capacity

Hold your phone toward a direction. See what's available.

---

## What you'll see

Each direction shows:
- A short description of the current pattern
- What this period suits (e.g., "good for slow conversations")
- What this period doesn't suit (e.g., "wait on big asks")

That's it. No long reports. No complicated charts.
Just a simple, real-time rhythm map.

---

## What Syncro is NOT

Syncro does not:
- Predict events
- Tell you fortune
- Promise outcomes
- Replace your own judgment

Syncro is a self-awareness tool.
It helps you notice your own rhythms.
The decisions remain yours.

---

## Why this matters

You already have natural rhythms.
You're more focused at certain times.
You're more open at certain times.
You feel friction at certain times.

Syncro helps you see those rhythms — not to obey them,
but to know them. To work with yourself instead of
against yourself.

---

## What Syncro draws from

Syncro's algorithms integrate:

- **Bazi (Four Pillars)** — millennia-old observations
  of how birth time correlates with personality patterns
- **Chinese Five Elements** — wood, fire, earth, metal,
  water as energy frameworks
- **Earthly Branches (12 Palaces)** — temporal/spatial
  patterns of energy
- **Modern chronobiology** — research on biological
  rhythms

These are frameworks for self-awareness, not predictive
systems.
