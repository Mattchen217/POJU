# Glyph

A 60-second mirror.

---

## What Glyph is

Glyph is POJU's free reflection feature.

When you have a question — about a decision, a stuck
moment, a moment of pause — Glyph offers you a different
angle.

You hold your question. You draw one of 100 archetypal
patterns, refined over a thousand years of human
reflection. The AI generates a structured report
(800-1100 words) based on the pattern, your question,
and your context.

Read with a wink. The patterns are mirrors, not prophecies.

---

## What Glyph reflects

The 100 patterns Glyph draws from come from one of the
oldest collections of human archetypes — refined over
millennia of reflection on the situations that humans
keep encountering.

These patterns describe common moments:
- Moments when momentum arrives
- Moments when paths open
- Moments when waiting matters more than action
- Moments when tension calls for recalibration
- Moments when external chaos reveals internal clarity

The patterns don't predict what will happen.
They describe what's already happening, often before
you've named it yourself.

---

## The 5 archetypal patterns

✦ **Divine Tailwind** — Aligned momentum.
   Conditions are moving in your direction. The hesitation
   itself is the work now.

✦ **Fair Sky** — Open paths.
   The way is clear. But clarity is an invitation, not a
   guarantee. You still walk it.

✦ **Still Water** — Patient depth.
   Below the surface, something is forming. Not forward,
   not backward — sit with what is.

✦ **Crosswind** — Tension as compass.
   The resistance you feel is not a wall. It's pointing
   you to ask a different question.

✦ **Eye of Storm** — Clarity at the still center.
   Around you, things are loud. Where you stand, it is
   quiet. This is where you can finally see.

---

## On the patterns

The five patterns are not labels of fortune.
They are not "good cards" or "bad cards."

Each one is a lens — a way of reading this particular
moment, for this particular question, held by this
particular person.

The same pattern can mean entirely different things on
different days, for different people, about different
questions.

What you receive is not a verdict.
It is a perspective — and an invitation to look more
carefully.

## Three rules

When using Glyph:

1. **One question per session.** Asking many things at
   once dilutes the response.

2. **If the same question calls you back, wait 48 hours.**
   Responses don't change just because you ask again.

3. **Compress your question into 60 characters.** The
   compression is the beginning of the response.

Free to use. No account. Take what's useful, leave
what isn't.
