## How POJU works

POJU is not a chatbot. It's an agent with a mission:
help you break through.

In a POJU session, the AI:

1. **Asks the right questions** — drawing on decision
   psychology, behavioral economics, and millennia of
   human reflection on hard choices.

2. **Listens for what you're actually saying** — including
   the contradictions, the avoidances, and the half-said
   things.

3. **Pulls threads you didn't know were connected** —
   linking your question to patterns and frameworks that
   open new angles.

4. **Holds you accountable to your own clarity** — not
   letting you settle for surface answers when you came
   for something deeper.

5. **Stays with you across sessions** — remembers what
   you explored, follows up on what you committed to,
   adjusts as your situation evolves.

The session ends when you say it ends — when you've seen
your way through, or you have a clear next step you can
actually walk.
