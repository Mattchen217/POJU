Filter already-understood labels to user-confirmed known or n_a items

Only project UnderstoodItem labels into already_understood when
user_confirmed_any is true, status is known or n_a, and the cleaned label
is non-empty. Unconfirmed, inferred, or merely covered items stay out of
the Decision Understanding already-understood list.

This does not change UnderstoodItem, Readiness, or Freeze rules, and does
not declare DI2 or M9 accepted.
