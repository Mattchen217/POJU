M5-R1: report charge authorization + client job binding

A report job no longer runs on an unconfirmed charge, and the client
binds a completed payload to the job it actually polled.

Charge authorization (new decision-report-charge.ts):
- Per-session durable record pending / authorized / refused /
  uncertain; every transition is a CAS on the exact record
  (MemoryKv compareAndSetExact, Upstash Lua), so one claimant charges.
- charge_ref (pass ledger idempotency ref) is fixed at the first claim
  and reused by every reconcile; the consume RPC returns
  already_consumed for a known ref, so a lost answer or a failed
  record write is reconciled without a second debit.
- Abandoned claims (60s) and uncertain outcomes are re-claimed and
  reconciled; Continue never opens a first charge.
- Per-job execution grant (job id, session, generation, charge_ref)
  written only for jobs of an authorized session.

Gates:
- start (new and reused) settles the authorization before any
  dispatch; a pending charge returns the job with
  worker=awaiting_charge; uncertain -> 503 CHARGE_UNCONFIRMED;
  refused -> abort + pass refusal.
- continue reconciles, then grants before reviving / dispatching.
- dispatchDecisionReportStage and ensureDecisionReportWorker refuse
  ungranted jobs (CHARGE_NOT_AUTHORIZED).
- runDecisionReportStage checks the grant at entry (every hop:
  background, relay, inline hand-off, legacy stage router) and
  fenceTouch re-checks it before every checkpoint / model call.
- abortDecisionReportJob revokes the grant first, stamps
  charge_refused (non-revivable), clears the dispatch marker.
- had_prior_job removed: a historical job is not a historical charge;
  Regenerate is free only with an authorized record.

chargeDeliveryPass marks rpc_error refusals as uncertain (legacy
callers unaffected).

Client: commitCompletedReportToSession takes the polled job_id;
verifyCompletedReport checks it, the session, and recomputes
report_job_id from session / Freeze / profile receipt / generation.
charge_refused surfaces as pass_required; CHARGE_PENDING keeps polling.

Tests: new scripts/test-m5-r1-authorization.ts
(pnpm test:m5-r1-authorization). M5 suite: A6 grants its directly
created job, B5 asserts the authorization record instead of the
removed charge marker. M3 suite drives the service + runner below
the route: its start() helper now authorizes the session and grants
the job (the step the route performs after a confirmed charge).
