    Privacy & boundaries. Straight talk.

    What stays on your phone or computer—and what this product never does.

    phonelink_lock

    Stays on this device

    Your chat and the report stay on the phone or computer you’re using right now—in this browser only. They are not uploaded as a chat history to our cloud. Want them gone? Clear this browser’s browsing data (or close a private window). Use a different phone, computer, or browser, and those chats won’t show up—even if you sign in with the same account. A new device means a new local copy—old chats don’t follow automatically; you only see them on the original device.

    vpn_key

    Your account is for PASS—not your chats

    Signing in is mainly so you can buy PASS and see your balance and receipts. It does not mean your conversations are saved to your account in the cloud. So if you open Eastern OS on another device, you’ll still see your PASS—but not the old chats from the first device. Want those chats again? Go back to that same device and browser—your account won’t sync them. PASS and chats are separate; don’t expect them as one cloud record.

    ios_share

    Keep a copy if you want one

    Need the conversation or report after you leave this browser? Before you clear browsing data or switch devices, email the report to yourself, or export a copy. That’s how you keep something you can open later and check against the path you clarified. The purchase email you get when you pay is a receipt—not a chat backup, and it won’t restore your dialogue. If you delete the local copy, we can’t bring it back—so take one with you here first; don’t wait until after you clear data or switch devices.
