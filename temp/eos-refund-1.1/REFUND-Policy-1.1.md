## 3. Eligible refund situations

Except for unused **purchased / one-time Buy Pass** refunded in product under §3.3 where that control is shown, refunds are **not automatic**. Eligibility for support-path categories requires a support review and, where relevant, technical verification against account / billing / fulfillment records. Submitting a support request does not guarantee approval.

### 3.1 Verified technical / fulfillment failure

You may be eligible for a refund (full or partial as appropriate to the charge) if we verify that:

- You were **charged**, but the corresponding **PASS entitlement was never issued** to your account ledger; or
- A **catastrophic pipeline failure** on our side (or our generation infrastructure) prevented **any** dialogue delivery for a PASS that was properly spent / initialized, such that the bundled entitlement could not be fulfilled in any usable form.

Ordinary slowdowns, temporary third-party outages that later resolve, user network issues, unsupported browsers, or incomplete user inputs do **not** automatically qualify. We will assess good-faith technical evidence.

**Request window:** Unless a longer window is required by applicable law or by the payment provider’s then-current rules, please submit technical-failure claims within **seven (7) days** of the charge or of the failed fulfillment event (whichever is later and reasonably knowable to you).

### 3.2 Duplicate identical charges

If our payment infrastructure registers **duplicate identical charges** for a single intended purchase action (same offering, same intended transaction), we will reverse the **extra** identical charge(s) after verification. Keep both receipt references so we can match them.

### 3.3 Unused purchased Buy Pass — self-serve in PASS details

For **purchased / one-time Buy Pass** remaining on your account ledger only (purchased / flex balance)—**not** subscription allotment:

A purchased Buy Pass remains **valid until used** (permanent until consumed under ledger rules). It is **not** subject to a short expiry window merely because time has passed since purchase.

While a purchased Buy Pass unit remains **unused**—meaning the associated decision dialogue was **never started / initialized** and that unit was **never consumed** on the ledger—you may refund it **in product** through **PASS details** (self-serve), for up to the **remaining purchased quantity** (you may select quantity from **1** through remaining purchased, where the product shows that control). There is **no twenty-four (24) hour (or other short-cap) request deadline** for this unused purchased Buy Pass path.

**How the money moves:** Each purchased Buy Pass unit maps to its own related original payment. Refunding **N** unused purchased units means **N** full refunds of those related original payments (typically oldest-first / FIFO by purchase)—**not** a single partial-amount refund of one combined charge. The dollar amount shown is the sum of those related full payments. Refunds return to the **original payment method**, subject to provider and banking rails. The payment provider executes the money movement; **timing follows the payment provider**—Eastern OS does not promise instant bank clearing.

**Subscription credits / allotment are not refundable through this PASS-detail path.** To stop future charges, cancel your subscription per §4. This path does **not** convert a monthly subscription allotment into a permanent one-time refundable credit, and does **not** create a mid-cycle pro-rata refund for subscription fees or unused subscription PASS.

Once a given PASS unit is consumed / dialogue has started, **that unit** is out of this unused purchased-PASS refund path. Change of mind or subjective dissatisfaction after consumption remains non-refundable except as §3.1 (verified technical failure), §3.2 (duplicate charges), or §3.4 (mandatory law / provider-required remedies) separately allow.

If the self-serve control is **unavailable**, **fails**, or the provider declines the monetary movement, contact **support@easternos.com**. Support remains available for technical failure, duplicates, mandatory law / provider-required remedies, and self-serve unavailability or provider failure.

### 3.4 Mandatory law / provider-required remedies

Nothing in this Policy limits **non-waivable** consumer or statutory rights that apply to you, or refunds that the payment provider / MoR is required to process under its then-current rules or applicable card-network / wallet policies. Where such a right applies, we will handle the request consistently with that obligation.

### 3.5 Verification

**Self-serve unused purchased Buy Pass (§3.3):** Where the PASS-details control is shown, eligibility is verified in-product against your ledger (remaining purchased quantity; unused / not dialogue-started) and the related original payments mapped to those units. Refunding N units triggers N full refunds of those related payments (typically FIFO)—not a partial-amount cut of one combined charge. You do not need a separate support review for that path unless the control is unavailable or fails.

**Support-path categories** (§3.1, §3.2, §3.4, and §3.3 when self-serve is unavailable or fails): We may request:

- Receipt / transaction / confirmation reference as shown on your checkout receipt;
- Account email or other account identifiers;
- Description of the failure;
- Screenshots, error messages, timestamps, and browser / device context.

Fraudulent or materially incomplete requests may be denied. Not every refund under this Policy requires a support review—only the categories that go through support.

## 4. Subscriptions — cancel anytime; no mid-cycle pro-rata solely for unused time

### 4.1 Cancel anytime

You may **cancel automatic renewal at any time** via the secure self-service billing / customer portal provided at purchase, or by contacting **support@easternos.com**.

### 4.2 Access through the end of the paid period

Upon cancellation:

- You retain access to PASS already allotted for the **current paid billing period** through the end of that period, subject to Terms and ledger rules;
- Cancellation stops **future** renewals; it does not, by itself, erase already allotted current-period PASS before period end.

### 4.3 Unused current-period PASS expire at period end

Unused PASS from the **current paid billing period expire when that period ends**. They do **not** become permanent one-time credits solely because you cancelled. Align this commercial rule with live Pricing / FAQ and the Terms.

### 4.4 No mid-cycle pro-rata refund solely for cancel / unused time / unused PASS

**No mid-cycle partial pro-rata refund** of subscription fees is authorized **solely** because you cancelled mid-period, did not use all PASS, or have unused time remaining in the paid period—except as this Policy separately allows (for example, verified technical delivery failure or duplicate charge) or as mandatory law / provider rules require.

## 6. How to request a refund

### 6.1 Unused purchased Buy Pass — primary path (PASS details)

If you have **unused purchased / one-time Buy Pass** remaining on the ledger, use the in-product **Refund unused PASS** (or equivalent) control in **PASS details** where the product shows it. That is the **primary path** for this category. See §3.3.

If that control is unavailable or fails, email **support@easternos.com**.

### 6.2 Other categories — contact support

For verified technical / fulfillment failure, duplicate charges, mandatory law / provider-required remedies, subscription billing help, or other situations under this Policy, email **[support@easternos.com](mailto:support@easternos.com)** (preferred for refunds and billing operations).

You may also use the [contact hub](https://www.easternos.com/contact). For privacy / data requests use **privacy@easternos.com**; for legal correspondence use **legal@easternos.com**.

### 6.3 What to include (support requests)

Please include:

1. **Receipt / transaction / confirmation reference** as shown on your checkout receipt or payment confirmation;
2. The **email / account** associated with the purchase (if any);
3. Whether the purchase was a **Buy Pass** or a **subscription** charge;
4. A clear description of the issue (technical failure, duplicate charge, self-serve unused Buy Pass control unavailable / failed, etc.);
5. Relevant **screenshots**, error messages, and approximate **date/time** (with timezone if known).

### 6.4 Processing targets

- **Self-serve unused purchased Buy Pass (§3.3):** The product initiates the related full refund(s) of the original payment(s) for the selected unused purchased quantity (N units → N full refunds, typically FIFO—see §3.3); the provider executes money movement. **Timing follows the payment provider**—we do not promise instant bank clearing.
- **Support-path requests:** We aim to **decide** valid, complete requests within approximately **two (2) business days** after receiving the information needed for verification; if approved, the monetary refund is executed through the payment provider / MoR under **provider timelines**, which may take additional banking or wallet processing days beyond our decision.
- We do **not** promise or guarantee a fixed bank clearing SLA beyond those provider timelines.

Incomplete support requests may be delayed until you provide missing references.

### 6.5 Windows summary

| Claim type | Request path / window (launch rule) |
|---|---|
| Unused purchased Buy Pass (never started / never consumed; remaining purchased qty) | **PASS details** self-serve **anytime while unused** (valid until used; see §3.3); support if self-serve unavailable / fails |

### 7.2 Unnotified chargebacks after delivery

If you initiate a chargeback or payment dispute **without** first contacting support, for a transaction where entitlements were **issued and/or delivered** in accordance with the Terms:

- Resolution may be **delayed** while we gather fulfillment records for the provider / network;

This is a conservative fraud/abuse response—not a theatrical permanent device blacklist. We cooperate with payment providers and networks as appropriate. Nothing here limits your non-waivable dispute rights under applicable law or card-network rules; it describes our account and entitlement response when abuse is reasonably indicated.
