## About This Report

### Your current state: ideas keep coming, but energy is spinning in vain
These past few years you've been charging forward, ideas one after another, actions never stopping, yet the results always fall just short. It's like an engine revving high but leaking fuel—energy is all consumed by anxiety, overthinking, and inner conflict, leaving very little to actually push things forward. You're tired not because you're not trying hard enough, but because your efforts are being dragged down by internal friction. Money isn't coming in not because opportunities aren't there, but because your state isn't ready to receive them.

### What this report offers: not predictions, but repair
This report won't tell you that you'll get rich on a specific date, nor will it give you a fixed script. Its job is to help you find where the engine is leaking—that is, where your energy is stuck, when your state will naturally improve, and before that, how to adjust your daily rhythm and mindset to stop spinning in vain. You can see it as a user manual, not a verdict.

### Change your approach: fix the engine first, then hit the gas
Continuing to push through with your current approach will only make the leak worse. You need to change your approach: stop and check the engine first, instead of flooring the accelerator. This means learning to do less when your energy is low, focusing your efforts on what truly brings returns, while giving yourself time to repair. When your state rises to a certain threshold, opportunities will naturally become easier to grasp. Until then, adjusting your stance is more important than blindly sprinting.
